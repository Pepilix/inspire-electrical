Commercial electrical work has to fit around a business that is either already trading or trying to open on a fixed date. Planning, communication and tidy, well-documented installations matter as much as the wiring.

## Typical commercial work

- **Office fit-outs and refurbishments**: small power, floor boxes, data containment, meeting room and kitchen supplies
- **Retail**: display and track lighting, tills and equipment, shopfront and signage supplies
- **Hospitality**: kitchens, bar equipment, feature and dimmed lighting, outdoor areas
- **Workshops and studios**: three-phase supplies, machinery isolation, task lighting
- **Distribution**: new or replacement distribution boards, sub-mains and submetering

## How we run commercial work

**Survey and scope.** We visit, review existing drawings and records, and agree what is in and out of scope in writing.

**Programme.** Shutdowns are planned and communicated. Phased work keeps parts of the premises trading.

**Site standards.** Risk assessments and method statements, tidy working areas and clear handover each day.

**Documentation.** Certificates, updated board schedules and as-installed information, so the next person who works on the installation can understand it.

For ongoing testing and planned maintenance, see [electrical maintenance](/services/electrical-maintenance/). For lighting in commercial premises, see [commercial lighting](/services/commercial-lighting/).
