Outdoor lighting changes how a house feels after dark. It also has to survive rain, frost, roots and garden tools for many years, which is why the parts you cannot see matter most.

## What we install

- **Path and step lighting** for safe movement around the garden
- **Uplighting** for trees, walls and planting
- **Wall lights** at doors, terraces and garages
- **Feature lighting** for water, sculpture and architecture
- **Outdoor power**: weatherproof sockets, supplies to garden rooms, gates, pumps and outdoor kitchens

## The details that last

**Cable and protection.** Cables are chosen for burial or exposure, protected where they run, and routes recorded so they can be found again. Outdoor circuits are protected by RCDs as the wiring regulations require.

**Fittings.** Fittings need an ingress protection (IP) rating suited to where they sit: a wall light under a canopy and an uplighter set into a lawn face very different conditions.

**Coordination.** We work with garden designers and landscapers so ducts go in before paving, and fitting positions are fixed before planting.

**Control.** Lighting is grouped so it can be used in sensible ways: the path on a timer, the terrace on a switch inside, feature lighting by scene.
