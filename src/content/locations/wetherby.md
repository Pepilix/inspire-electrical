## Wetherby and the Inspire studio

The Inspire group's studio is at Thorp Arch Estate, a few minutes from Wetherby town centre. Inspire Electrical brings electrical contracting into the same group as Inspire Audio Visual, which has designed and installed smart home, lighting and cinema systems since 2009.

## Homes around Wetherby

Wetherby sits on the River Wharfe where Leeds, Harrogate and York meet, and the A1(M) runs alongside it. The housing ranges from the stone-built town centre and riverside streets to large detached houses in Linton, Collingham and Sicklinghall, and family homes on newer estates around the town.

## Local authority boundaries

Wetherby is in the Leeds City Council area of West Yorkshire, while Kirk Deighton, North Deighton and Harrogate to the north are in North Yorkshire. That matters for building control and planning queries, and we know which authority applies to your address.
