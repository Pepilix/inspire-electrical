## 2. Inspire family references

### 2.1 trustinspire.com (group site)

Full access. This is the strongest source for family resemblance.

- **Colour:** warm charcoal `#231f20` (taken from the logo artwork), warm paper `#f1eee8`, brass `#b8976a` used only for lines and nodes.
- **Type:** Cormorant Garamond headings, Inter body, IBM Plex Mono for annotations (indices, specification schedules, title blocks).
- **Graphic language:** borrowed from technical drawings: hairline rules, crop marks on photographs, dimension lines, a drawing title block, a "specification schedule" for project data.
- **Motion:** headline lines rise from a mask; a brass rule draws across the three services; photos reveal with a clip wipe; a brass path fills down the projects list as you scroll and lights a node per project. Everything is disabled under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **Interaction:** service panels widen on hover/focus on desktop; burger menu on phones.
- **Positioning:** "Smart homes, electrical work and care home WiFi". Inspire since 2009; Inspire Electrical described as "the newest part ... opening in 2027".

**Adopt for Inspire Electrical:** the palette, the three typefaces, the drawing-sheet graphic language and the restrained, content-led motion. These make the family link immediately recognisable.

**Differentiate:** trustinspire is a calm gateway. Inspire Electrical is a lead-generation site. It needs its own accent, much stronger contact paths, service depth and local SEO architecture.

### 2.2 inspire-av.com

Seen only via one mobile screenshot of the live homepage.

Observed:

- Logo: "INSPIRE / AUDIO VISUAL" lock-up with a square monogram, black on white.
- Light (white / off-white) page ground, unlike trustinspire's dark ground.
- Cormorant-style serif headline ("Three disciplines, one team."), Inter-style body copy.
- Small label pattern: a black box with serif caps ("WHAT") followed by letter-spaced sans caps ("WE DESIGN & INSTALL").
- Header on mobile: phone and email icons on the left, logo centred, burger on the right. Direct contact is one tap away.
- A persistent "Contact us" button pinned to the bottom of the screen.
- Large single-image service tiles with a serif service name ("Smart Home") and a domain pill ("inspire-av.com").

**Adopt:** one-tap phone and email in the mobile header; a persistent but quieter contact action on mobile; the letter-spaced eyebrow label.

**Avoid:** the floating red button colour (no brand rationale for red in the family palette) and overlap of the floating button with content.

## 6. Opportunities

These are reasoned from the verified search landscape and the brief. They are not based on competitor traffic data, which was not available.

1. **Project-led credibility.** Directory results cannot show project depth. Proper case studies (scope, challenges, drawings, photographs) with a specification schedule format are a gap for architect, developer and premium homeowner searches.
2. **Specialist intent pages.** Architectural lighting, lighting control, new-build electrical and garden lighting are under-served by directories and generalist sites. These are lower-volume but higher-value searches, and the Inspire group's background makes them credible.
3. **Useful local pages.** Directory town pages are near-identical templates. Location pages written around each town's real housing stock (Harrogate's Victorian and Edwardian stone villas, York's terraces and listed buildings) answer real questions and avoid the doorway-page problem.
4. **Answer-first advice.** EICRs, consumer unit replacement and Building Regulations notification generate genuine questions. Sourced, plain-English answers support the commercial pages through internal links.
5. **Group linkage.** Inspire Audio Visual and trustinspire.com are existing, relevant domains. Correct cross-linking and `Organization` / `subOrganization` structured data tie the businesses together for search engines.
6. **Trust parity.** Competitors lead with scheme registration and reviews. Inspire Electrical must reach parity before launch (see `docs/verification-checklist.md`). This is a requirement, not an opportunity.

## 7. Competitor review checklist (to complete in a browser)

For each competitor, record:

- Homepage H1, title tag and meta description
- Primary navigation items and service list
- Location pages: count, URL pattern, uniqueness of content
- Phone number placement (header / sticky / footer)
- Scheme logos, review widgets, guarantees
- Schema types present (use the Rich Results Test)
- PageSpeed Insights mobile score and Core Web Vitals field data (if available)
- Google Business Profile: category, review count, photos
